Current series

Priced In

In progress · 2026–2027

Climate data shown in the visual language of a trading screen: warming stripes drawn as candlesticks, a CO₂ ticker tape, Hong Kong temperatures set against interest rates. The series asks what it means when the future of the planet is "priced in."

Finished pieces will appear here as they are completed.

About

I'm Joe Lam, based in Hong Kong. My background is in global markets and I'm a certified ESG analyst (CESGA). That mix shapes my work: I use public climate, sustainability and market data as raw material, and try to make it something people can feel, not just read.

All data in my work comes from public sources, credited with each piece.
