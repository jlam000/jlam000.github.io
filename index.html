Current series

Priced In

In progress · 2026–2027

Climate data shown in the visual language of a trading screen: warming stripes drawn as candlesticks, a CO₂ ticker tape, Hong Kong temperatures set against interest rates. The series asks what it means when the future of the planet is "priced in."

Finished pieces will appear here as they are completed.

About

I'm Joe Lam, an ESG professional in Hong Kong on a mission to become an ESG influencer, making sustainability impossible to scroll past.

The numbers behind climate change shouldn't stay buried in reports. I turn them into art that makes people stop, look, and start talking.

Every piece uses public data, and every source is credited.
